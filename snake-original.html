Why this fits the chat app

This game is intentionally self-contained. It can sit inside a Games tab, modal, side panel, or a dedicated route without interfering with your existing chat JavaScript. The high score is saved locally for the device. Later, the score can also be sent to your Apps Script backend so members can compete on a shared leaderboard.
