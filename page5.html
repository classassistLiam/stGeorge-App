A simple operating guide

How it works

A practical, transparent way to care for shared youth resources.

1. Register every item

Give each item a unique asset ID. Add its category, purchase date and value where known, current location, custodian and condition.

2. Keep status current

Use Available when ready for use, Assigned when issued to a person or team, Under repair when unavailable due to a fault, and Retired when removed from service.

3. Record maintenance

Log inspections and repairs with dates, details, responsible person and cost. Keep receipts and supporting records separately.

4. Export for accountability

Export CSV files for committee review, handovers and periodic stocktaking.

Data storage note: This standalone prototype stores records in this browser on this device using local storage. It does not yet sync between phones, computers or committee members. For shared live records, connect it to your approved Google Sheets / Apps Script or Firebase backend and add user authentication and role permissions.
