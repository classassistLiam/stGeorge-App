# Upgrade notes

## Visual direction
St George Youth now has a public landing experience and a member-facing module gateway. The design borrows the clarity of a modern product dashboard while keeping the church's own identity: forest green, light neutrals, restrained lime accents and editorial typography.

## Deliberately not changed
The four recovered original modules were not redesigned internally. Their embedded application logic is large and backend-sensitive. Preserving them avoids repeating the risk of losing working functions during a visual-only rewrite.

## Before production replacement
The source audit documents current remote files that are not identical to the recovered originals in this archive. Compare and reconcile those live files before deploying this package over the current production project.
